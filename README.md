# Wine-Insight-Dashboard
This dashboard provides a detailed view of wine sales dynamics across Australia, focusing on operational efficiency, product performance, customer segments, and city-level revenue targets. It combines freight analytics, product-level insights, packaging behavior, and market performance to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🚀 Project Objectives

The goal of this BI project is to explore:

- Product performance across wine sub-segments
- Freight logistics by priority & cargo mode
- Regional revenue performance vs sales targets
- Packaging type impact on revenue & shipment volume
- Revenue vs order quantity correlation
- Sub-segment contribution by quantity and revenue

---

## 📊 Key Dashboard Components

### **1. Freight Analysis by Priority & Quantity**
- Compares order volume across priorities (Critical, High, Medium, etc.)
- Analyzes freight mode usage (Air, Cargo, Road)
- Shows operational dependency on cargo logistics

### **2. Top Products by Revenue & Quantity**
- Scatter plot highlighting SKU performance
- Shows which products produce high revenue vs high volume

### **3. Top Sub-Segments**
- Ranks wine sub-segments by revenue
- Identifies Pinot Gris, Riesling, Sauvignon Blanc as top performers

### **4. Sales Target by City**
- Compares city-level revenue against predefined sales target
- Canberra and Bunbury exceed revenue expectations

### **5. Sub-segment Order Quantity**
- Treemap illustrating order volume per wine sub-segment
- High volume vs high revenue differences reveal pricing effects

### **6. Packaging Time Analysis**
- Compares revenue and order quantities by packaging type
- Large packaging types contribute significantly to revenue

---

## 🧠 Insights Summary

- Revenue concentration exists in a few premium wine sub-segments
- Cargo logistics are crucial for fulfilling high-priority orders
- Certain cities significantly outperform revenue targets
- Packaging strategies influence profitability and throughput
- Revenue does not always correlate with order volume across SKUs
